Social Media Checklist



The DO's

* Do set your personal accounts to private to control who can see your personal life and photos.
* Do use professional profile pictures on platforms like LinkedIn and GitHub to make a strong first impression.
* Do credit original creators when sharing articles, images, or research to maintain academic and digital integrity.
* Do think before you post; ask yourself if you would be comfortable with a future employer or professor seeing the content.
* Do use two-factor authentication (2FA) on all social accounts to protect yourself from identity theft.



&#x20;

The DON'TS

* Don’t post confidential information such as your registration number, home address, or phone number on public forums.
* Don’t engage in cyberbullying or use offensive language in comments, as this can have serious disciplinary consequences.
* Don’t share unverified news or "fake news" that could cause panic or misinformation within the student community.
* Don’t click on suspicious links or "free giveaway" ads on social media, as these are often phishing attempts.
* Don’t post photos or videos of others without their explicit consent, respecting their digital privacy.
