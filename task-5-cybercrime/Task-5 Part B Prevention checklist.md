**Stay Safe Online: A Guide for Students**

As a Digital Ambassador, I recommend following these 8 actionable steps to protect your identity and finances:

* Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA): Always use 2FA on your email, social media, and banking apps to add an extra layer of security beyond just a password.
* Verify Before Sharing Documents: Never share copies of your Aadhaar, PAN, or College ID on unverified WhatsApp groups or Google Forms for "jobs" or "scholarships".
* Use Strong, Unique Passwords: Avoid using your name or birthdate; instead, use a mix of symbols, numbers, and capital letters for every account.
* UPI Safety - PIN is for Sending Only: Remember that you never need to enter your UPI PIN to receive money. If someone asks you to enter your PIN to claim a prize, it is a scam.
* UPI Safety - Check the Payee Name: Before hitting 'Pay' on any UPI app, double-check the verified name of the recipient to ensure you aren't sending money to a fraudulent account.
* Avoid Public Wi-Fi for Banking: Do not log into your bank accounts or make UPI payments while connected to free public Wi-Fi at cafes or stations.
* Check URL Legitimacy: Always look for the "https://" and the padlock icon in your browser address bar before entering any personal information on a website.
