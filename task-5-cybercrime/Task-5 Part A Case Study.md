**Case Study: Digital Identity Theft**

&#x20;

**What the Crime Is?**

Identity theft occurs when a cybercriminal wrongfully obtains and uses another person's personal data (such as Aadhaar details, PAN, or login credentials) for fraud or other crimes.



**How it Typically Happens (Step-by-Step)**

* The Hook: A student receives a message on WhatsApp or Telegram claiming they have won a "Digital Ambassador" scholarship or a high-paying part-time remote job.
* Information Gathering: To "process" the application, the attacker sends a professional-looking Google Form asking for a photo of the student’s College ID, Aadhaar card, and a "verification" selfie.
* The Takeover: Using these documents, the criminal performs a SIM Swap or bypasses KYC (Know Your Customer) protocols at a digital bank to open a fraudulent account in the student’s name.
* The Exploitation: The stolen identity is used to take out instant "micro-loans" or to launder money from other UPI scams, leaving the student legally responsible for the debt.



**Who is Targeted?**



College students are primary targets because they often have clean credit histories and are frequently looking for internships or online income opportunities. And, even the migrant or the poor workers are targeted by the employers on the pretext of job and using their Aadhar card and PAN card for registering a fake company.



**The Consequences:**

The victim faces severe financial and legal distress. The student may find their bank accounts frozen by the cyber cell, their credit score (CIBIL) ruined before they even graduate, and they may be called in for police questioning regarding transactions they never authorized.





**REAL CASE BASED SCENARIO**



**Case Study: Identity Theft of a Migrant Worker (December 2025)**

**What the Crime Is?**

Identity theft involves the unauthorized use of a person's personal identification information—such as Aadhaar, PAN, or bank details—to commit fraud or other crimes. In this case, a worker's documents were used to create a "shell" financial identity to hide massive illegal transactions.



**How it Happened (Step-by-Step)**

* **The Deception:** In 2019, a worker named Karan Kumar Ram from Bihar was hired for a low-paying data entry job in Delhi.



* **Document Harvest:** His employer asked for his Aadhaar and PAN cards under the pretext of opening a "salary account". He was even persuaded to change his Aadhaar address to a local one.



* **The Misuse:** After only three months of work, he was dismissed and paid in cash. Unknown to him, his documents were then used to register a fake company.



* **The Discovery:** Over three years later, in July 2025, Ram began receiving notices from the Income Tax Department across multiple states (Karnataka, Haryana, Bihar).



* **The Scale:** Investigations by the Delhi Police Economic Offences Wing (EOW) revealed that the company created in his name had carried out transactions worth ₹48 crore.



**Who was Targeted?**

The target was a vulnerable migrant worker looking for basic employment. Scammers often target individuals who may not regularly monitor their tax or credit profiles.



**The Consequences:**

The victim faced a "legal nightmare," including a criminal investigation by the EOW and massive tax liabilities for income he never earned. An FIR was registered in December 2025 for cheating by personation and forgery.
